Help and support

Need help?
Email stearnsappstudios@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and the game version (Book → Settings → About). We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Book → Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Family Sharing members get the unlock too.

Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone only. There are no accounts and no cloud saves, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

Can I start over?
Yes: Book → Settings → Start over. This can't be undone.

The sound is off.
Check Book → Settings → Sound, and your iPhone's silent switch: the game stays quiet when your phone is on silent.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
