Privacy Policy

Fizzwick’s Potion Shop · Effective December 1, 2026

Short version: the game doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party software that collects data. It doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (such as Sound on/off) are saved on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device and isn't sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock full game" purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details; Apple only tells the game whether the unlock was bought. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we don't collect any personal information, we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13 or anyone else.

Support emails

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and we don't share them. You can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
